Denver Crime Awareness Center

Denver Crime Awareness Center Site-plan

  1. Site Name:

    Denver Crime Awareness Center

  2. Site Purpose:

    The purpose of the website is to promote community involvement and support for policing by making them aware of the amount and types of crime occurring in their neighborhoods. It will also provide them a way to report safety concerns.

  3. Scenarios:

    • How many crimes were reported in my neighborhood in the last week?
    • What types of crimes were reported in my neighborhood?
    • What does all of this mean?
    • How can I report safety concerns?
