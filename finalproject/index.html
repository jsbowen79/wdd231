Denver Communities Fighting Crime through Crime Awareness

There were an average of 19.6 violent crimes per day in Denver in 2024. During the same time period, Denver experienced an average of 94.2 property crimes per day.1 This works out to over 110 crime victims for each day of the year.

"Crime Prevention Cannot work without you. The importance of crime prevention comes from the involvement of the community working together with law enforcement..."
--Richard Arrington2

The Denver Crime Awareness Center is committed do reducing crime through education and community involvement. We strive to provide you Denver's most up to date crime data to decrease your chances of becoming one of those 110 daily crime victims.
